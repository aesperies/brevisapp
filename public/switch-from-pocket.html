Why we built this

Newsletters are everywhere

I was drowning in newsletters. Dozens came in every day—from investors, founders, engineers, and thought leaders. I wanted to stay informed, but I didn't have hours to read them. I'd save them to Pocket, promise myself I'd read them later, and never did.

That's when I realized the problem: I didn't need to save articles. I needed someone to read them for me and tell me what matters.

Brevis was born from that frustration. We built it for professionals who care about staying informed but value their time even more.
