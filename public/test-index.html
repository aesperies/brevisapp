QUESTIONS

Quick answers

What is BREVIS?

▼
BREVIS is an AI-powered newsletter management platform that summarizes, organizes, and extracts insights from your newsletters automatically. It works with any newsletter provider and delivers a personalized daily digest to your inbox.

How does the AI work?

▼
Our AI uses advanced natural language processing to analyze newsletter content, identify key themes, extract actionable insights, and create concise summaries. The system learns from your reading patterns to prioritize content you care about most.

Do I need a credit card for the trial?

▼
No. Our 15-day free trial requires no credit card. Just sign up and start using BREVIS. You'll only be charged after your trial ends, and only if you choose to upgrade.

What formats can I import?

▼
BREVIS supports 100+ newsletter platforms including Substack, Morning Brew, The Verge, LinkedIn newsletters, Medium, LinkedIn, and any email-based newsletter. You can also upload OPML files or manually add RSS feeds.

Can I cancel anytime?

▼
Yes. You can cancel your subscription at any time with one click. No contracts, no hidden fees, no questions asked. If you cancel, your account remains active through the end of your billing period.

Standard vs Premium: what's the difference?

▼
Standard gives you the essentials: unlimited newsletters, AI summaries, smart tagging, and daily digests. Premium adds Knowledge Graph visualization, executive briefs for sharing with teams, and team collaboration for up to 5 people.
