# BREVIS - Brand Guidelines

## 📐 Logo

### Files Included
- `logo.svg` - Full logo (black on white or transparent)
- `logo-white.svg` - White version (for dark backgrounds)
- `favicon.svg` - Favicon for web

### Logo Specifications

**Primary Logo: BR Monogram**
- Style: Geometric, minimalist (Nothing-inspired)
- Letters: B and R combined
- Design: Clean strokes, rounded curves
- Format: Square (1:1 ratio)

**Minimum Size:**
- Web: 48x48px
- Print: 15mm x 15mm

**Clear Space:**
- Maintain space equal to height of "B" on all sides

### Logo Usage

✅ **Do:**
- Use on solid backgrounds (black or white)
- Maintain aspect ratio
- Use provided SVG files
- Scale proportionally

❌ **Don't:**
- Distort or stretch
- Add effects or shadows
- Change colors (only black or white)
- Rotate or skew
- Place on busy backgrounds

## 📐 Logo Construction

The BR monogram consists of:

**B Letter:**
- Vertical stroke (left)
- Two curved strokes (right)
- Top curve: smaller
- Bottom curve: larger

**R Letter:**
- Vertical stroke (left)
- Curved top (right)
- Diagonal leg (bottom-right)

**Spacing:**
- Letters: Connected but distinct
- Stroke width: Consistent (8px at 200px size)
- Curves: Smooth and round

**Inspiration:**
- Nothing Phone branding
- Bauhaus geometry
- Swiss typography
