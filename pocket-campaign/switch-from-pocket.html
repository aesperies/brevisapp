Frequently Asked Questions

How does Brevis actually read my newsletters?
When you forward a newsletter to your Brevis address, our system extracts the content and sends it to Claude AI, Anthropic's most capable language model. Claude reads and understands the newsletter in context, then extracts 4-6 key insights as concise, actionable bullet points. You get a summary that captures what matters most — without the fluff.
Is my data safe? What happens to my newsletters?
Your data is encrypted in transit and at rest. We never sell your data or use it to train models. Your newsletters are processed securely through Anthropic's API and stored only for the time needed to generate your digest. You can delete your account and data at any time. We comply with GDPR and SOC 2 standards.
Can I import my old Pocket articles into Brevis?
Pocket allows you to export your saved articles before shutdown. While Brevis is designed for forward-looking newsletter management rather than archiving past articles, we're building import tools for our Premium users. For now, focus on forwarding your current newsletters to Brevis and start fresh with a cleaner, smarter system.
What happens after the 3-month promotional period ends?
After 3 months, your subscription will automatically renew at the regular price ($12/mo for Professional, $29/mo for Premium). We'll send you a reminder before the first full-price charge. You can cancel anytime, no questions asked. No contracts, no lock-in.
Do you support languages other than English?
Yes! Brevis is bilingual: English and Spanish (Español). Summaries are generated in the language of the original newsletter. Premium users can also request summaries in a different language. We're planning support for French, Portuguese, and German in Q3 2026.
