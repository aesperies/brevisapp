## 1. PRICING STRATEGY (THE CORE DECISION)

### The Pricing Crisis at Current Rates

**BLUF:** Current pricing ($8/$10/month) creates an impossible math problem. Raise to $12/$29/month immediately.

#### Current Pricing Math (Broken)
- Pro tier: $8/month
- Premium tier: $10/month
- Blended average (assuming 50/50 split): $9/month
- Revenue needed: $10,000/month
- **Paying customers required: 1,111**

This is unrealistic for a bootstrapped product with no brand recognition competing against Readless ($4.90) and Mailbrew ($4.92).

#### Why Professionals Can Bear Higher Pricing

The core value prop is **opportunity cost savings:**

| Professional Type | Hourly Rate | 30 Min/Day Savings | Monthly Value |
|---|---|---|---|
| In-House Counsel | $200-300/hr | $100-150 | $2,000-3,000 |
| VC Partner | $250-400/hr | $125-200 | $2,500-4,000 |
| Strategy Consultant | $200-350/hr | $100-175 | $2,000-3,500 |
| Legal Associate | $150-250/hr | $75-125 | $1,500-2,500 |

If Brevis genuinely saves professionals 30 minutes daily (highly realistic given current UX), the value is **$2,000-4,000/month per user**. Charging $12-29/month is capturing 0.3-1.5% of value—a bargain users will recognize.

**Competitive Pricing Context:**
- Superhuman (email): $30/month (enterprise version: $50/mo)
- Shortwave (AI email): $25/month
- Readwise Reader (generalist): $8.99/month
- Notion (document tool): $10-20/month
- GitHub Copilot: $20/month

Professional tools in the $15-30/month range are standard. Readless and Mailbrew are consumer-positioned, not professional tools.

### Recommended Pricing Structure

**BLUF:** Launch with three tiers. Annual pricing offers 2-months-free discount to lock in revenue.

#### Monthly Pricing

| Tier | Price/Month | Users Per Tier | Audience | MRR at Scale |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Free** | $0 | 4,500 | Entry-level + evaluation | $0 |
| **Pro** | $12 | 350 | Individual professionals | $4,200 |
| **Premium** | $29 | 150 | Teams + power users | $4,350 |
| | | **500 total paying** | | **$8,550** |

**Blended ARPU at scale:** $17.10/month → $10,060/month at 590 customers (15% buffer)

#### Annual Pricing (Launch with annual option)

| Tier | Price/Year | Savings | Effective Monthly |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Pro Annual** | $120 | Save $24 (20% discount, nets 2 months free) | $10 |
| **Premium Annual** | $290 | Save $58 (20% discount) | $24.17 |

**Annual pricing impact:** Increases LTV by 40%, improves cash flow, reduces monthly churn sensitivity.

### Pricing Justification: Why $12/$29 Works

1. **Captures Value Without Guilt**
   - Professionals earning $200+/hour won't blink at $12/month for 30 min/day savings
   - Legal and VC verticals are accustomed to $20-50/month SaaS tools
   - Current pricing ($8/$10) sends "cheap" signal—attracts bargain hunters with high churn

2. **Reduces Churn, Improves Metrics**
   - Premium pricing attracts committed users (self-selection)
   - Users investing $12+ have higher engagement and lower churn
   - Reduces support burden (premium tier users require less hand-holding)

3. **Improves Unit Economics**
   - CAC payback at $12/month: ~8-10 months (acceptable for SaaS)
   - CAC payback at $8/month: ~12-15 months (unacceptable bootstrapped)
   - LTV:CAC ratio improves from 1.5:1 → 3:1

4. **Enables Real Marketing**
   - With $12/month, can invest in paid acquisition ($20-30 CAC)
   - With $8/month, even organic acquisition feels expensive

5. **Competitive Positioning**
   - Positions Brevis as a professional intelligence tool (like Readwise Reader + Substack Pro)
   - Not racing to the bottom with Readless ($4.90)
   - Differentiates from consumer-tier newsletter apps

### Transition Strategy for Existing Users

**BLUF:** Grandfather 14 test users at $8/month until Month 6. New signups start at $12/$29.

- **Days 1-3:** Notify test users of new pricing effective Month 6
- **Month 6:** New pricing takes effect for test users (or they can stay on old pricing forever)
- **All new signups:** Start at $12/$29 from day one
- **Expected revenue impact:** Month 1-6 = minimal; Month 6+ = full pricing

## 6. RISK REGISTER

### Top 5 Strategic Risks

#### Risk #1: Pricing Resistance
**Severity: MEDIUM | Probability: 40%**

**Description:** Despite competitive justification, legal/VC professionals may resist jump from $8/$10 to $12/$29.

**Symptoms:**
- Conversion rate drops >50% from current free-to-paid
- Churn on existing test users >20% when new pricing announced
- Common feedback: "Too expensive vs. Readless"

**Mitigations:**
1. Grandfather test users at old pricing until Month 6 (reduces churn risk)
2. Emphasize value not price: "Saves 30 min/day = $2,000-4,000/month in opportunity cost"
3. Offer annual pricing ($120/year) as 20% discount (psychological anchor)
4. A/B test messaging: run low-price cohort vs. value-based cohort; measure LTV
5. Have fallback: $15/$25 pricing ready if adoption stalls

**Owner:** Antonio (pricing decision), Agent (A/B testing execution)

---

#### Risk #2: Competition Undercutting
**Severity: HIGH | Probability: 60%**

**Description:** Readless, Mailbrew, or new entrant drops pricing to $2-4/month in response to Brevis' $12/$29.

**Symptoms:**
- Competitor announces price cut <30 days after Brevis launches at $12
- Price comparison articles on ProductHunt: "Brevis costs 3x Readless"
- Free-to-paid conversion drops <3%

**Mitigations:**
1. **Differentiation first:** Specialize in legal/VC/consulting verticals. Readless doesn't have legal expertise.
2. **Lock-in strategy:** Annual pricing with longer lock-in reduces churn when price wars erupt.
3. **Network effects:** Build Slack community ("Brevis Legal Professionals") so switching costs increase.
4. **Do not race to bottom:** Cutting price to $8/$10 would be death spiral. Stick to $12/$29.
5. **Quick vertical expansion:** If threatened in Pro tier, emphasize Premium tier ($29) for teams.

**Owner:** Antonio (strategy), Agent (monitor competitive moves, alert Antonio within 24h)

---

#### Risk #3: Product/Market Misfit
**Severity: MEDIUM | Probability: 35%**

**Description:** Lawyers/VCs don't actually want AI summaries; they prefer human curation (HackerNews) or existing competitors (Readwise).

**Symptoms:**
- Free-to-paid conversion <2% despite good signup numbers
- Churn >15%/month after first week
- User feedback: "Summaries miss important nuance" or "I prefer reading originals"
- NPS <20

**Mitigations:**
1. **Validate early:** Month 1-2, conduct 10+ user interviews: "How are you using Brevis? Why did you churn?"
2. **Iterate summarization:** If feedback is "summaries miss X," update Claude prompt to include X.
3. **Offer "original + summary" mode:** Let users toggle between AI summary and original email.
4. **Pivot if needed:** If legal/VC doesn't work, try academia (researchers), finance (analysts), healthcare (doctors).
5. **Kill criteria:** If churn >20%/month and NPS <10 after 4 weeks, pause acquisition; focus on product.

**Owner:** Agent (user interviews, NPS tracking), Antonio (pivot decision)

---

#### Risk #4: Founder Burnout / Time Constraint
**Severity: MEDIUM | Probability: 30%**

**Description:** Antonio can't find time to drive Phase 1 founder sales or handle crises while managing BITKRAFT.

**Symptoms:**
- Phase 1 ends with <10 paying customers (due to zero founder sales)
- Security breach occurs; no owner to respond
- Key partnership opportunity missed because Antonio is too busy
- Repeated tasks (like weekly metrics) pile up

## 8. DECISION CHECKPOINTS & COURSE CORRECTIONS

### What to Do If Things Aren't Working

#### If Free-to-Paid Conversion <2% After Week 2
1. **Pause acquisition.** Stop all outbound sales emails.
2. **Root cause:** Conduct 3-5 user interviews. Why did free users not convert?
   - Possible reasons: price shock, summarization quality, onboarding confusion
3. **Quick fix options:**
   - Offer extended trial (30-day vs. 14-day)
   - Lower price to $15/$25 temporarily (A/B test against $12/$29)
   - Improve summarization (add more newsletter sources, better prompts)
4. **Decision deadline:** End of Week 3. Either proceed with fix or escalate to Antonio.

#### If Churn >10%/Month After Week 2
1. **Survey churned users.** Email template: "What could we improve?"
2. **Common reasons & fixes:**
   - "Summaries not accurate" → Improve Claude prompt
   - "Too expensive" → Offer annual pricing discount
   - "Didn't have time to use it" → Simplify onboarding, auto-create first digest
3. **Iterate & relaunch:** Test fix on next 5 signups before declaring "solved."

#### If No Founder Sales After 2 Weeks (0 of 20 targets responded)
1. **Adjust outreach:** Current email too template-y? Try adding personal story.
   - Example: "Jane, I spent 15 years reading legal newsletters daily. Then I realized: I was paid $250/hr to... triage emails. That's insane. So I built Brevis."
2. **Expand target list:** Ask test users for intros to colleagues.
3. **Try different channels:** LinkedIn message (warmer than cold email), Twitter DM, conference networking.

#### If Payment Failures >5% of Transactions
1. **Immediate:** Email all customers with failed payments; offer manual invoice.
2. **Root cause:** Check Stripe logs. Is it card validation? 3D Secure? Rate limiting?
3. **Fix & retest:** Work with Stripe support; re-run payment for all failed accounts.

#### If Server Uptime <99%
1. **Escalate immediately:** This breaks trust, especially for paid users.
2. **Root cause:** Database query too slow? Summarization API timeouts? Memory leak?
3. **Temporary fix:** Disable slowest feature (e.g., multi-email summarization) if needed.
4. **Long-term fix:** Database indexing? Caching? Move to managed DB service?
