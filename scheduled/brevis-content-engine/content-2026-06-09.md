That gap — between what we sign up for and what we actually read — is the problem Brevis was built to close.

Here's the use case that made it click for me. Instead of 23 separate emails competing for attention across my week, Brevis reads all of them and sends me one brief each morning. Same sources. The result isn't "more content consumed." It's the opposite. It's 30+ minutes a day I'm not spending triaging an inbox, and zero guilt about the folder I was ignoring.
