## LinkedIn Post

Use case that sold me on the feature I use most in my own product:

Last week I was prepping for a diligence call on a gaming studio. The kind of call where missing one regulatory shift can cost the fund a deal — or worse, miss a risk that surfaces in year two.

Nine newsletters relevant to the space had landed overnight. Gaming policy. EU crypto regs. Unity/Unreal licensing terms. Creator economy shifts. 90 minutes of reading I didn't have — the call started in 40.

This is the exact problem I built Brevis for.

The feature I leaned on: the 5-bullet summary. Each newsletter condensed into five bullets — key claim, supporting data, the counterargument, the "so what for me," and a one-click link to the original if I wanted to dig deeper.

I scanned all nine in twelve minutes. Flagged two for a full read later. Walked into the call current, calm, and with the relevant headlines loaded.

The insight that made me keep building: most newsletter content isn't under-written — it's over-formatted for the way professionals actually consume information. You don't need 1,400 words. You need the five that change your decision.

Brevis does that job in the background so your morning belongs to you.

Professional $12/mo. Premium $29/mo. Bilingual EN/ES. Link in comments.
