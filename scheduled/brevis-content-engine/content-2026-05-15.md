## LinkedIn Post
Friday reflection from the trenches.

I started building Brevis because I was drowning. As in-house counsel at a VC fund, my inbox is a battlefield — deal flow, regulatory updates, portfolio company news, market intelligence. Industry research says the average professional gets 121 emails a day and subscribes to 23 newsletters. Under 20% of those newsletters actually get read.

That's not a discipline problem. That's a volume problem.

This week, less code and more listening. Two things keep surfacing from the people I'm building for:

First, they don't want another inbox. They want triage that respects the one they already have.

Second, they don't trust "AI summaries" on faith. They want the summary AND a one-click path back to the source, because in regulated work the source is the only thing that holds up.

So that's where the focus is going. Less novelty, more trust.

Building in public means publishing the quieter weeks too — the ones where you didn't ship a flashy feature but you sharpened the thinking. That was this week.

If you've ever opened your inbox on a Monday and felt the dread, you're the person I'm building this for. Tell me — what's the one newsletter you actually keep paying attention to, and what makes it stick when everything else gets archived unread?
