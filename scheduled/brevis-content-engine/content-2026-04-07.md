## LinkedIn Post

**I subscribe to 23 newsletters. I used to read maybe 4.**

Not because the others weren't valuable — they were. I just didn't have 45 minutes every morning to wade through walls of text before my first deal call at 9am.

As in-house counsel at a gaming and web3 VC fund, missing a regulatory update isn't just inconvenient. It's a liability. Last month, I almost missed a critical SEC staff bulletin on token classification that directly impacted a portfolio company's token side letter. It was buried in paragraph 11 of a fintech newsletter I'd been "saving for later" for three days.

That's the moment I knew Brevis had to exist.

Here's what the feature actually does: Brevis connects to your inbox, identifies your newsletters, and uses Claude AI to generate structured bullet-point summaries — prioritized by the topics you care about. Not generic summaries. Summaries tuned to your role, your industry, your priorities.

For me, that means token regulations, IP licensing developments, and AI governance updates surface at the top. For a consultant, it might be market sizing data and framework updates. For a founder, funding announcements and competitor moves.

The result: I now process all 23 newsletters in under 8 minutes. Every morning. Before coffee gets cold.

We're in soft launch right now, and early users are reporting 30+ minutes saved per day. If you're drowning in newsletters but afraid to unsubscribe because you might miss the one thing that matters — that's exactly the problem we built Brevis to solve.

Check it out at brevisapp.com. I'd love your feedback.
