## LinkedIn Post
Monday productivity reset.

Last Monday I opened my inbox and counted: 121 emails. 23 of them were newsletters I had subscribed to with the best intentions — the AI roundup, the gaming industry weekly, the VC deep-dive, the legal trends digest.

I read maybe four of them. The rest accumulated quiet guilt until Sunday night, when I'd "catch up" by deleting them all in batch.

That's the open secret of professional reading. Most of us are subscribed to far more than we consume. We pay for it in two ways — the Sunday-night dread, and the slow drift away from the field-edge insight we signed up for in the first place.

This is why I started building Brevis.

Not another reader app. Not another inbox-zero trick. Just one job: read everything you subscribed to, summarize what matters, hand it back in five minutes a morning.

Last week I got 30+ minutes of my weekday back. More importantly, I actually know what's in those newsletters now. The signal was already there. The friction was the problem.

Monday productivity is not about doing more. It's about reclaiming the things you already decided were worth your attention — and removing everything in between.

Try it: brevisapp.com
