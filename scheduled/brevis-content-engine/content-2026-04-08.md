## LinkedIn Post

**I almost missed a $4M deal because of an unread newsletter.**

Last year, buried in email #87 of 121 that day, was a market analysis flagging a regulatory shift in token governance. I didn't read it. A week later, it became the central issue in a term sheet negotiation — and I was behind.

That was the moment I decided to build Brevis.

I'm a VC lawyer. My days are spent on SAFTs, token side letters, and IP licensing across gaming and web3. I subscribe to 23 newsletters because staying informed isn't optional in this space. But the math doesn't work: 121 emails/day, 23 newsletters, and maybe 20% actually get read.

So I started building — nights and weekends at first, now with real momentum. Brevis uses Claude AI to distill every newsletter into structured bullet points. The key insight, the action item, the trend — surfaced in seconds instead of buried in paragraphs.

What I've learned building in public as a non-technical founder:

Your first users are people exactly like you. I built Brevis for lawyers and VCs who can't afford to miss signals. That specificity is a feature, not a limitation.

Ship before it's pretty. Our early users don't care about pixel-perfect design. They care that they saved 30 minutes this morning.

Building a product while doing your day job is brutal — but it means you're your own best beta tester, every single day.

We're in soft launch now. If you're a professional drowning in newsletters, I'd love your feedback.
