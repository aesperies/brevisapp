## LinkedIn Post
It's Monday. Your inbox already has 121 new emails waiting (industry average). You're subscribed to 23 newsletters. Statistically, you'll read fewer than 20% of them.

I know this because I live it. As in-house counsel at a venture fund, my inbox is the front door for deal flow, term sheets, market signals, and regulatory updates. Missing the wrong newsletter can mean missing a thesis-shifting trend. Reading them all is impossible.

So I built Brevis. Every newsletter gets summarized into a tight bullet list before I open it. The hypothesis was simple: if I can decide in 15 seconds whether a piece earns 5 minutes of my attention, I get back the 30+ minutes a day I was burning on triage.

It worked — not because the AI is magical, but because most newsletters bury their one good insight under 800 words of context.

Try one experiment this week: take your 3 most valuable newsletters and force yourself to write a 3-bullet summary BEFORE reading the full text. You'll learn two things fast — what's actually signal vs. noise, and how often a headline overpromises the body.

If you'd rather have an AI do that triage for you in the morning, brevisapp.com.
