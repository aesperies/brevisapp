Not because of a productivity hack. Because the 23 newsletters she'd signed up for over the years — the ones that piled up unread, made her feel behind, and quietly added to the 121 emails landing in her inbox every day — finally stopped being a chore.

That's the whole reason Brevis exists. Bilingual, built for professionals who are drowning in subscriptions they meant to read.
