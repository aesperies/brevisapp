## LinkedIn Post

Two weeks into the Brevis soft launch. Here's the honest scoreboard:

47 signups. 9 paid conversions at $12/mo. 1,840 newsletters processed. Roughly 94 hours of reading time returned to our users.

The number that matters most to me isn't any of those. It's this: the average professional subscribes to 23 newsletters and actually opens 4 of them. The other 19 sit in an inbox, generating guilt and occupying mental real estate. I know because I was that professional — a lawyer drowning in 121 emails a day, convinced I was missing something important in the stack I never touched.

Brevis doesn't ask you to read more. It asks you to read less, better. Claude reads the 19 you were going to ignore and hands you back the three bullets that actually matter. If nothing matters, it tells you that too.

The biggest lesson from week two: users don't want a "smarter feed." They want permission to stop pretending they'll get to it later.

We're shipping a bilingual (EN/ES) digest format next week — the first specialist tool for global professionals who read across two languages. If you've been on the fence, this is a good week to try it.

brevisapp.com

What's one newsletter you keep subscribing to but never read? Curious what's sitting in your guilt pile.
