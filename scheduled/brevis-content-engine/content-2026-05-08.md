## LinkedIn Post
It's Friday, and I'm doing my weekly reflection in public.

When I started building Brevis, I had a simple problem: I was subscribed to 23 newsletters and reading less than 20% of them. The rest piled up in a folder I labeled "later" — a folder I never opened.

My first instinct was to unsubscribe. But the truth is I didn't want fewer newsletters. I wanted the signal without the time cost. The information was good. The format wasn't.

That's the thesis behind Brevis: bullet-point summaries from Claude, delivered in the time it takes to drink your morning coffee. 30+ minutes back every day. Bilingual EN/ES. Built for professionals who have to stay current but can't afford to read 121 emails before lunch.

This week's reflection: building in public is harder than building in private, but every reply, every DM, every honest piece of feedback shapes the product faster than any roadmap document I could write alone.

What's next: deeper coverage for legal, VC, and consulting verticals. Better Spanish language coverage. And continuing to ship so anyone considering this path can see how it actually goes.

If you're a professional drowning in newsletters and curious about a calmer way to consume them, brevisapp.com.

What's the one newsletter you read religiously? Curious to hear. Drop it in the comments.
