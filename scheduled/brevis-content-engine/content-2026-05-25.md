6:47 AM Monday. Coffee in hand. I opened my inbox and counted.

121 emails. 23 newsletter subscriptions. Substack, Axios, Stratechery, three different VC weeklies, two AI roundups, the legal trade press. All things I "wanted" to read.

That's 2.5 hours a week. 10+ hours a month. Spent on the meta-work of email, not the work itself.
