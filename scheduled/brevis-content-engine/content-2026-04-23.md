## LinkedIn Post
Something shifted in the newsletter ecosystem this year, and most professionals haven't caught up.

Five years ago, subscribing to a newsletter was a conscious choice. You'd read a piece you liked, sign up, and treat each issue as a small commitment. Fast-forward to 2026: the average knowledge worker is subscribed to 23 newsletters and reads less than 20% of them.

We didn't get lazier. The ecosystem industrialized.

Creator platforms made it trivial to launch a newsletter. LLMs made it trivial to produce one daily. Embedded signup forms made it trivial to capture you in a moment of enthusiasm that wouldn't survive the next Tuesday morning.

The inbox absorbed the blast — and so did our attention.

What I've noticed working in VC: the professionals who stay ahead aren't reading more. They're reading differently. They've shifted from "open and skim" to "summarize and scan." They don't read the newsletter — they read the signal inside it.

That shift is why we built Brevis. Not to replace your subscriptions. To route them. One digest, bullet points, your morning back.

The newsletter ecosystem isn't broken. The reading layer is. 2026 is the year that gets fixed.

Curious where you land: unsubscribe, ignore, or summarize?
