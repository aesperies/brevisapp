Friday reflection from a week of building.

I subscribe to 23 newsletters. I was reading less than 20% of them.

The math wasn't working. Smart writers' best thinking landing in my inbox - and most of it ignored, because there is no 45-minute window before the first call of the day.

So three weeks ago I started using Brevis on my own subscriptions. English market reads in the morning. Spanish industry news with the afternoon coffee.

What changed in one week:

- 121 emails/day now get triaged before I touch the inbox
- Newsletter reading dropped from ~45 min to under 10
- 30+ min/day back to deal work

The thing nobody tells you about information overload: you are not behind because you are slow. You are behind because the format hasn't caught up to your day.

A 2,000-word essay made sense when reading was the work. Now reading is the prep for the work. The format has to compress to fit the calendar.

Brevis is not trying to replace deep reading. It is trying to make sure the signal reaches you before the day buries it.
