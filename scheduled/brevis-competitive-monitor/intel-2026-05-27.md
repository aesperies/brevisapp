# Competitive Intel — Week of 2026-05-27
*(Tier 2 this week: Superhuman / Grammarly rebrand)*

---

## [URGENT] — Headline of the Week

**Grammarly has rebranded as Superhuman and launched "Superhuman Go" — a unified AI suite at $12/month Pro, the exact same price point as Brevis Pro.** The new entity bundles Grammarly (writing), Coda (workspace), Superhuman Mail (inbox), and Superhuman Go (100+ AI agents covering Google Workspace, Salesforce, Outlook, Jira, Confluence, and third parties).

This is the biggest competitive event since the Anthropic-legal entry on May 12. It hits Brevis on three vectors at once:

- **Price collision:** Superhuman Pro = $12/mo. Brevis Pro = $12/mo. A buyer comparing the two now sees "writing + workspace + mail + 100 agents" for $12 vs. "AI newsletter digest" for $12. Brevis's entry tier needs sharper vertical positioning or a price re-think.
- **Distribution scale:** Grammarly's installed base is ~40M+ DAU. Every one of them now sees Superhuman Go inside their existing tool. Brevis cannot match that surface area; it has to be visibly *not* the same product.
- **Mail surface:** Superhuman Mail is now inside the suite. The "AI in your inbox" narrative — which Brevis was using as a wedge against Gmail Gemini — is now also being claimed by a $12 horizontal incumbent.

**Recommendation:** This week's bilingual (EN/ES) post should be *"Why $12 for a vertical AI tool beats $12 for a horizontal AI suite"* — explicit head-to-head positioning against Superhuman Pro, naming it, with the in-house counsel / VC use case as the proof point. The Anthropic-legal post from last week stays in queue; this Superhuman post takes priority because the price comparison is happening *right now* in buyer evaluations.

- **[High] Grammarly rebranded as Superhuman; launched Superhuman Go.** Suite = Grammarly + Coda + Mail + Go (100+ AI agents including Google Workspace, Outlook, Salesforce, Jira, Confluence connector agents). Pricing: Free / Pro $12 / Business $33 / Enterprise custom. Pro tier is a direct price-match against Brevis Pro at $12/mo, but with 4 bundled products and a 40M+ DAU distribution channel. - **[High] Meco free-tier audio CONFIRMED.** Meco's own documentation and the readless.app pricing landing page both now list "audio news summaries" as a free-plan feature, with the "Daily Brief audio podcast" reserved for PRO at $34.99/yr. - **[Low] No new news at Readless product side** beyond the SEO content engine.

## Opportunities for Brevis

- **Head-to-head with Superhuman Pro at $12.** No competitor is going to publish "Why our $12 vertical tool is better than Superhuman Pro" — this is Brevis's space to claim. Cleanest angle: "Superhuman optimizes the inbox; Brevis replaces newsletter overload entirely." Bilingual.
- **In-house GenAI at 87% adoption** is now a third-party validated stat Brevis can cite on the landing page and in cold outreach. Pair with the Artificial Lawyer "next act is in-house productivity" quote from May 12 for stronger social proof than any single-vendor citation.
- **Meco free now matches Brevis free** — but Meco free does *not* speak Spanish natively and has no vertical (legal/VC) curation. Re-anchor Brevis's free tier story on EN/ES + vertical curation, not on "we have audio."

## Threats

- **[High] Superhuman Pro at $12/mo** with bundled writing + workspace + mail + 100 agents — direct price collision with Brevis Pro, vastly larger distribution base, materially more product surface. Brevis's $12 tier needs a sharper "why this and not Superhuman" story within 30 days or it gets quietly defeated in buyer evaluations.
- **[High] Meco free feature parity confirmed** — closes the entry-tier wedge as warned last week; revisit the "Brevis Lite at $5" floor proposal from the May 6 report this week, not next quarter.
- **[Medium] Legora's $600M Series D** signals that the well-capitalized legal-AI tier (Harvey, Legora, plus a possible Anthropic-legal product) is consolidating ad budget, hiring, and channel — Brevis's content marketing has to lean harder into niches those vendors will not serve (solo GC, micro-LP fund counsel, EN/ES bilingual practitioners).
