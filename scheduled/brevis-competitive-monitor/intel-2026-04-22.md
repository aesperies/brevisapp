# Competitive Intel — Week of 2026-04-22

**BLUF**
- [Medium] **Inoreader just launched BYO-LLM** (OpenAI, Mistral, Anthropic + your own API key) on its Pro tier ($7.50/mo annual). This is the most direct feature pressure on Brevis's AI summarization moat we've seen this quarter.
- [Medium] **Superhuman is now a Grammarly product** (Oct 2025 acquisition) and shipped Auto Drafts / Auto Labels / Auto Archive in Q4 2025. Grammarly's distribution behind a $30/mo email-AI tool changes the air cover for the AI-inbox category, even though Superhuman doesn't directly target newsletter readers.
- [Low] **Mailbrew is dead-on-the-vine** (free forever, no AI summarization, owned by Readwise since 2023) and **Pocket has shut down** — Matter is hoovering up displaced users with a free tier. Two real openings for Brevis to recruit "orphaned" newsletter readers.

---

## Significant Changes This Week

- **Inoreader Intelligence — multi-LLM choice (April 2026 launch).** Users can now pick OpenAI, Mistral, or Anthropic, and bring their own API key. Pro is $7.50/mo annual ($90/yr) or $9.99 monthly. ([Inoreader blog](https://www.inoreader.com/blog/2026/04/choose-your-ai-platform-for-intelligence-features.html))
- **Sora shutdown announced** for April 26, 2026 — not directly competitive, but it confirms the "burn-rate doesn't justify usage" thesis for over-engineered AI consumer apps. Worth referencing in Brevis positioning around lean ops. ([the-decoder](https://the-decoder.com/openai-sets-two-stage-sora-shutdown-with-app-closing-april-2026-and-api-following-in-september/))
- **Q1 2026 was a record VC quarter** — $300B globally, 80% of it AI. Expect more well-funded entrants in adjacent spaces (AI inbox, AI feed, AI summarization). ([Crunchbase](https://news.crunchbase.com/venture/record-breaking-funding-ai-global-q1-2026/))

## Opportunities for Brevis

1. **Pocket-orphan / Mailbrew-stagnation capture.** Pocket shut down (Matter is taking the read-later refugees). Mailbrew is owned by Readwise but has no AI summarization. Both audiences map cleanly to Brevis's value prop. Run a targeted "switch from Mailbrew/Pocket" landing page in EN+ES. ([Matter on X](https://x.com/matter/status/1925646585779585416))
2. **Vertical specialization is still wide open.** None of the competitors named today specialize for legal, VC, or compliance professionals. Brevis at $12/$29 sits between consumer ($5-10) and enterprise ($30+); the legal/VC vertical justifies the premium and avoids head-on price competition with Readless and Inoreader.
3. **The "47 sources, 2.3 hours/day" pain stat is repeating in coverage.** Use it in headline copy, paid acquisition, and the founder narrative — it's currently un-claimed by competitors as a positioning hook.
4. **BYO-LLM is now table stakes.** Inoreader just normalized it. Brevis should at minimum publish a roadmap commitment (or ship a "Powered by [your model choice]" toggle for Premium tier).
5. **Audio is heating up.** Meco, Matter, and Readwise Reader all now ship daily-brief or article audio. Brevis already has a Kindle audio path — push that as a differentiator (long-form audio for serious professionals, not just 5-min bites).

---

## Threats

- [Medium] **Inoreader's BYO-LLM** at $7.50/mo annual undercuts Brevis Pro by ~$4.50/mo and lets power users sidestep Brevis's AI-cost argument entirely. Most acute for technically-fluent prospects.
- [Medium] **Grammarly + Superhuman.** Grammarly will push Superhuman into its 30M+ DAU base. Superhuman is not a direct newsletter competitor, but it absorbs "AI in my inbox" mind-share and budget at the prosumer tier.
- [Medium] **Readwise Reader's Ghostreader.** Document-aware chat + library-wide highlight Q&A is a deeper PKM (personal knowledge management) experience than what Brevis ships today. Bundle pricing ($9.99 covers Reader + Readwise) is sticky.
- [Low] **Shortwave Tasklet** (Slack/Notion/Asana/HubSpot integrations). Confirms that "summarize then route" is the next layer up. Brevis should plan a roadmap item for Slack/Notion delivery of digests.
- [Low] **Funded Q1 entrants.** No specific newsletter-AI seed/Series A surfaced this week, but $242B of AI VC in Q1 means a well-capitalized newcomer is a matter of when, not if. Watch Y Combinator S26 and W26 batches closely.

---

## Recommended Actions

1. **Publish a "vs. Inoreader Intelligence" comparison page within 2 weeks.** Lead with vertical depth (legal/VC), curated source quality, and bilingual EN/ES — not raw model choice. Explicitly acknowledge BYO-LLM is on the roadmap if it is.
2. **Ship a Pocket-and-Mailbrew migration landing page** (`/switch`) in EN+ES with a one-click email forwarding setup and a "first digest in 24 hours" promise. The audience is actively shopping right now.
3. **Add audio to the marketing message.** Brevis's Kindle audio integration is differentiated — but it isn't on the homepage or in any current outreach copy. Surface it.
