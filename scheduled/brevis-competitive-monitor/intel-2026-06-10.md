# Competitive Intel — Week of 2026-06-10
*(Tier 2 this week: Feedly)*

## BLUF

- **Feedly (Tier 2 rotation) is visibly moving upmarket** — its real money is now the Enterprise "Market Intelligence" tier (from $1,600/mo), with consumer reading deprioritized and AI summarization (Leo) locked behind Pro+ at $99/yr. That leaves the consumer/professional curated-digest lane *more* open, not less. [Low]
- **One new well-resourced legal-vertical entrant: LawVu launched "LegalOS" June 2** — an AI workspace for in-house legal teams (Estée Lauder, Arsenal FC on the roster). It's a workflow OS, not a briefing, so it's adjacent rather than head-to-head, but it's the best-funded thing to land in Brevis's legal buyer's field of view this week. [Medium]
- **Gartner now predicts legal-tech budgets will double by 2028** (May 26 press release). A clean, citable tailwind stat for the Brevis legal-vertical pitch. [Low]

## Significant Changes (delta from last report)

- **[Low] Feedly (Tier 2, index 4) — upmarket pivot is the real story.** Current pricing: Free (100 sources) → Pro $72/yr ($6.99 monthly) → Pro+ $99/yr ($12.99 monthly, the only tier with Leo AI) → Enterprise Market Intelligence **from $1,600/mo**. Feedly is now positioned as a security/competitive-intelligence platform (customers cited: Airbus, Cloudflare, Delta Dental), not a consumer reading tool. Not a direct Brevis competitor. The signal: the most established RSS/AI-reader brand is walking *away* from the individual professional reader toward enterprise intel seats — confirming the curated-digest-for-professionals lane Brevis occupies is being vacated at the top, not crowded.
- **[Medium] LawVu "LegalOS" launched June 2.** AI-powered operating system for in-house legal teams; enterprise logos (Estée Lauder, Zumba, Arsenal FC, BVNK). This is a workflow/matter-management OS, not a daily briefing — different job — but it targets exactly Brevis's legal-vertical buyer (in-house counsel) with real funding behind it. Not a collision this week; worth watching for any "daily brief / current-awareness" feature creep that would move it into Brevis's lane.
- **[Low] Gartner: legal-tech budgets to double by 2028** (May 26). New third-party tailwind stat, distinct from the adoption-rate stats tracked in prior weeks (87% FTI/Relativity, 52% Thomson Reuters). Budget-growth framing is more useful for the "this category is funded and growing" part of the Brevis pitch than for the "everyone's adopting" part.
- **[Low] Gmail AI Inbox — continuity, no escalation.** Confirmed rolling out to US English-language users; same trajectory as last week, no new product surface or pricing move. The High-priority Gmail counter-post recommended the last three weeks remains the single highest-leverage content item if it has not yet shipped. No *new* delta this week — status unchanged.
- **[Low] Readless — no material change.** $4.90/mo held. Same SEO content-engine pattern (new pricing-comparison posts for Feedly, Inoreader, The Rundown this week). No product change.
- **[Low] Meco — no material change.** $34.99/yr Pro held; monthly option $3.99/mo. Free tier (mailbox, groups, bookmarks, audio summaries, offline) unchanged. 4.77★ / 660+ Play Store reviews. No new news.

## Opportunities for Brevis

- **Feedly's upmarket retreat is a positioning gift.** The clearest incumbent in AI-assisted reading is repricing itself for $1,600/mo enterprise intel seats. Brevis can claim the "curated daily briefing for an individual professional — without an enterprise contract" space more credibly than a month ago. Useful contrast point in landing copy and on the Feedly comparison page.
- **LawVu / LegalOS validates the legal-vertical thesis without competing for the same job.** Their launch is free top-of-funnel proof that in-house legal teams are buying purpose-built AI. Brevis's angle: "LegalOS runs your matters; Brevis keeps you current on everything outside them." Co-existence framing, not defense.

## Threats

- **[Medium] LawVu LegalOS** — well-funded, same buyer, adjacent job. Not a threat today; becomes one only if it adds a current-awareness/briefing feature. Flag for next legal-vertical rotation.

## Recommended Actions

1. **Confirm the Gmail AI Inbox counter-post (EN+ES) has shipped — if not, ship it this week.** It has been the top recommendation three weeks running; Gmail's rollout is the most time-sensitive narrative window Brevis has. No new reason to wait.
2. **Add a Feedly comparison/positioning page** built on the "curated digest for one professional, no enterprise contract" contrast now that Feedly's entry price for AI is $99/yr and its real product is $1,600/mo enterprise intel. Low effort, clean organic-search target.
3. **Log LawVu LegalOS to the legal-vertical watchlist** and re-check at the next legal rotation for any briefing/current-awareness feature. No action beyond monitoring this week.
