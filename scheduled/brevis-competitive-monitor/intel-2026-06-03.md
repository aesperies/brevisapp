- **[High] Gmail AI Inbox shipping, not just announced.** Last week the threat was the May 20 dark-pattern opt-out narrative. - **[Medium] Shortwave new pricing structure (Tier 2 rotation).** Tiers now: Free (with new "Sent with Shortwave" footer on outbound), Personal (gmail.com / .edu only, lower-priced — positioned for home/school users), Pro (full AI Search + AI Write in your voice, 3 accounts), Business ($24/mo annual, up to 10 accounts, 5-year searchable history), Premier ($36/mo), Max ($100/mo). Not a direct Brevis competitor — Shortwave is Gmail-on-steroids, not a digest — but the friction added to the free tier confirms the broader AI-email pricing trend: free is being quietly devalued.

Adds a calendar-driven content beat to Brevis's legal vertical for the next 8–10 weeks. Spanish-language coverage of the EU AI Act is thinner than English coverage, so the EN+ES bilingual angle is a real edge.
- **[Medium] Corporate legal AI adoption: 23% → 52% in one year (Thomson Reuters / Streamline.ai coverage this week); 60%+ of in-house teams expect to depend less on outside counsel.** Different methodology than the FTI/Relativity 87% stat cited last week, but same directional story. - **[Low] Readless — no material product change.** Same SEO content engine; new pricing-comparison landing pages this week for Readwise, Matter, The Information. Pattern unchanged from prior weeks.
- **[Low] Meco — no material change.** Pricing held ($34.99/yr Pro, free + audio confirmed). No new news.

## Threats

Brevis's "5-minute daily briefing" pitch sits directly on top of Gmail's new "personalized briefing with recommended to-dos." Content this week needs to define the boundary clearly (curation vs. inbox automation), or buyers will assume Brevis is "the thing Gmail just did."
- **[Low] Shortwave free-tier downgrade.** Not a direct hit, but it confirms a category trend: AI-email free tiers are getting friction. Useful as a contrast point in Brevis's free-tier positioning, not a defensive issue.
- **[Low] HeyNews voice-matched drafting.** Adjacent to a different buyer (newsletter creators). Defer to next Tier 2 rotation; revisit if funding lands.
