Every Monday at 8:47am, I used to do the same thing.

Open inbox. See 14 unread newsletters from the weekend. Feel that familiar flicker of guilt. Star three of them. Promise myself I'll "read them tonight."

I never did.

The professional's information problem isn't access — it's triage. The average knowledge worker receives 121 emails a day and subscribes to 23 newsletters. Fewer than 20% are ever actually read. We're not underinformed. We're buried.

Here's the productivity tip I wish someone had given me five years ago: your job is not to read everything. Your job is to *extract signal* from everything.

That reframe changed how I built Brevis. We don't help you read more newsletters — we help you stop pretending you're going to. Every morning, one brief. Bullet points. The three things that actually matter from the 14 things that landed overnight. English or Spanish. Thirty minutes back on your calendar before your first meeting.
