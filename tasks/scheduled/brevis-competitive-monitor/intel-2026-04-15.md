# Competitive Intel — Week of 2026-04-15

## BLUF
- **Superhuman acquired by Grammarly (Oct 2025)** — new users must buy Grammarly Business ($33–40/mo) to access email. Creates customer displacement; opportunity for Brevis-adjacent positioning on "AI productivity without vendor lock-in."
- **Shortwave shipped Tasklet (Oct 2025)** — cross-app AI automation (Slack/Notion/Asana/HubSpot) on top of email; $9M raised (Lightspeed, USV). They are the pace-setter on velocity (96 features in 2024).
- **No urgent shutdowns this week.** Mailbrew remains effectively sunset post-Readwise acquisition; Readwise Reader is consolidating share with Ghostreader chat + audio reviews at $9.99/mo.

---

## Significant Changes

- **Superhuman + Grammarly**: Pricing restructured — Starter $30/mo, Business $40/mo, annual 17% off. New purchase path routes through Grammarly Business. Auto Drafts, Auto Labels, Auto Archive now standard. [Medium] impact on premium end of the market.
- **Shortwave — Tasklet launch**: Full AI automation layer; uses OpenAI + Anthropic (Claude) with RAG-based semantic search. Expanding beyond email into workflow. Direct threat to any "AI assistant for professionals" narrative.
- **Readwise Reader**: Ghostreader chat sidebar is now first-class on web. "Chat with Highlights" lets users query their entire library. Audio Reviews (podcast-like) shipped. PDF reparse upgraded. Still $9.99/mo.
- **Feedly**: No pricing changes detected in last 30 days. NewsReader tier unchanged (Free / Pro $6.99 / Pro+ $12.99). Multilingual AI + automated newsletters still locked behind $1,600+/mo enterprise tiers — **persistent gap** for mid-market.
- **Readless (direct competitor)**: Aggressive SEO content strategy — dozens of "vs" and "pricing 2026" comparison posts ranking against Readwise, Feedly, Inoreader, Pocket, Meco. Pricing remains ~$4.90/mo. They are winning on organic discovery.
- **Mailbrew**: Still effectively dormant post-Readwise acquisition (2023). No meaningful 2026 updates. Users continue migrating to Readwise Reader.
- **Yupp.ai shutdown** (Apr 2026): Not a direct competitor but a signal — $33M seed startup shut down from lack of PMF. Reinforces that AI-content tools without clear vertical focus are fragile.

## Opportunities for Brevis

1. **[High] Vertical specialization still uncontested.** None of the incumbents target legal, VC, or gaming verticals. Readless wins horizontal SEO but has no vertical playbook. This is Brevis's moat — double down on legal/VC newsletter taxonomy and source curation.
2. **[Medium] Bilingual (EN/ES) is a true white-space.** None of the monitored tools offer first-class Spanish. Feedly's multilingual AI is gated at $1,600/mo. Brevis's EN/ES positioning is a defensible differentiator for LATAM + US-Hispanic professional market.
3. **[Medium] Superhuman's Grammarly bundling creates displacement.** Users paying $40/mo for email are a ripe audience for "$29/mo for the intelligence layer your inbox can't give you." Consider a wedge campaign: "Superhuman reads your email. Brevis reads your industry."
4. **[Low] Readwise "Chat with Highlights" is a feature to mirror.** If Brevis adds chat-over-archive, it closes a feature gap without needing to match Readwise's full reader surface.

## Threats

- **[High] Shortwave + Tasklet velocity.** They ship monthly, are well-capitalized, and now cover workflow automation. If they add newsletter curation, they overlap Brevis materially. Watch for any "digest" or "summary" feature in their changelog.
- **[Medium] Readless SEO dominance.** They will own horizontal discovery queries. Brevis cannot compete on generic "newsletter AI" keywords — must win vertical queries ("AI newsletter for lawyers", "VC newsletter digest", "resumen newsletters en español").
- **[Medium] Readwise Reader's bundle.** $9.99/mo for Reader + highlights + AI chat + audio is very strong value. Brevis's $12 tier must feel clearly differentiated.
- **[Low] Yupp.ai-style shutdowns.** Signals VC skittishness on horizontal AI content tools without PMF — reinforces urgency of hitting paid conversion milestones before the next funding-winter narrative takes hold.

## Recommended Actions

1. **Ship a "Superhuman users" comparison landing page** this week — target the displaced audience with a concrete side-by-side. Low effort, high leverage given the Grammarly disruption.
2. **Audit Brevis source taxonomy for legal/VC/gaming** and publish a "Best legal newsletters 2026" + Spanish equivalent post. Directly contest Readless's SEO strategy on terrain they don't cover.
3. **Add Shortwave's changelog to weekly competitive monitor scope.** Their velocity makes them the single biggest medium-term threat; quarterly monitoring is not enough.
